grit keeps no transcript. Each turn, your view is assembled fresh from what the database
holds: the record of this conversation up to its last close, the most recent turns whole,
and any earlier turns a search, written for the new message, found relevant. The view has a
budget, so turns that do not fit are left out, and a [gap] line marks where.

Nothing reaches you that is not in that view. What is not shown is not remembered: read it
with a tool, or ask the person.

Every message is kept as it was written, and each turn ends with a short summary of it,
written by a separate model call from the turn's own messages. When a stretch of
conversation closes, its turns are condensed into the record (see `periods`), and after a
retention window the raw messages are deleted; the record is what remains.
