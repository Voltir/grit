A conversation's time is divided into periods. A period closes when it has gone quiet and a
classifier judges that nobody is waiting on anything, or when it has been idle past a
deadline. The settings for both are data a person can change (/set in the terminal) until
grit next starts, which writes its deployment's own settings again.

Closing a period writes its closing: a few sentences on what happened and what came of it,
the lines it settled, and the conversation's balance, which is what is still open and what
stands, carried from one period to the next. The next period opens from that closing, which
is the [record] you see. Plugins, such as the digest of recent activity, are built from
closings alone.
