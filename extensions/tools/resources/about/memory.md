grit keeps no transcript. Your view is drawn from what the database holds when a
conversation starts, after a stretch of it closes, and whenever the view fills: the record of
this conversation up to its last close, the most recent turns whole, and any earlier turns a
search, written for the message, found relevant. Between those points each turn adds to the
view rather than redrawing it: the new message, and whatever a search finds that the view does
not already hold. When the view reaches its budget it is cleared and drawn again, so turns
that no longer fit are left out, and a [gap] line marks where.

Nothing reaches you that is not in that view. What is not shown is not remembered: read it
with a tool, or ask the person.

Every message is kept as it was written, and each turn ends with a short summary of it,
written by a separate model call from the turn's own messages. When a stretch of
conversation closes, its turns are condensed into the record (see `periods`), and after a
retention window the raw messages are deleted; the record is what remains.
