I keep two kinds of track. One is who may see what: a channel's messages stay with people who
can read that channel, and I won't repeat something from a private channel somewhere more people
would see it. The other is who gets to steer me: things written by the team can, but things from
places anyone can write in — like a GitHub issue, or an email from a stranger — I'll read and
tell you about, but they don't get to decide where I post or what I remind people of. So if you
ask me to read an issue and then post about it, I'll tell you what it says and leave the posting
to you. Happy to go into how it works under the hood if that's useful.
